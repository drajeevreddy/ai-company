## Blog Post Templates

Each blog post follows this structure:
1. SEO title tag (<60 chars) with primary keyword
2. Meta description (150-160 chars) with primary keyword
3. H1 heading with primary keyword
4. Introduction paragraph (hook + keyword)
5. 3-5 H2 sections with H3 subsections
6. FAQ section (3-5 questions)
7. CTA to book appointment
8. Internal links to doctor pages and other blog posts
